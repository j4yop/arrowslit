# Design principles

Arrowslit is built from scratch for the Mac notch.

## What it is

- A narrow, vertical aperture for the few things needed right now: time, the
  next event, what you copied, a thought, the machine's pulse.
- A local-only core with no accounts, analytics, ads, subscriptions or telemetry.
- Native controls and a restrained surface that stays out of the way.

## What it avoids

- Copying another app's layout, dimensions, colours, copy or animation curves.
- Network accounts, analytics, ads, subscriptions or telemetry.
- Private APIs at launch.
- A giant grid of every possible widget. The rail stays small and legible.
- Claiming ownership of the notch; the notch is the surface, not the brand.

## Our point of view

The notch is the smallest useful screen on the Mac. Arrowslit treats it like an
aperture: a narrow opening that gives you a full view of the few things you need
right now without opening a full app.
