Arrowslit 0.1.0
=============

A local-only notch utility for macOS 14 or later.
Universal build: Apple Silicon and Intel.

Homebrew (recommended)
-----------------------
    brew install --cask j4yop/tap/arrowslit

Homebrew installs the app without the quarantine flag, so it opens normally.

Manual install
--------------
1. Open Arrowslit-0.1.0.dmg and drag Arrowslit into Applications.
2. Open Applications and double-click Arrowslit. macOS will refuse the first
   launch because Arrowslit is not notarized yet. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security and scroll down. Click
   "Open Anyway" next to Arrowslit, confirm with Touch ID or your password,
   then click "Open Anyway" once more.
4. Settings opens automatically. Choose the features you want and close the
   window. Arrowslit keeps running in the notch.

If you prefer Terminal, after dragging the app into Applications:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Arrowslit.app"
    open "/Applications/Arrowslit.app"

Using it
--------
Hover the top of the screen for 0.4 seconds, or click the strip, to open.
Click the slit in the menu bar if the strip is hard to reach. Settings can be
opened again from the menu bar or by reopening Arrowslit.

Privacy
-------
Arrowslit has no account, analytics, or network access.
Clipboard history, notes, the file tray and timers stay on this Mac.
