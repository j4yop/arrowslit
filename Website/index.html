Make the notch yours.

Choose the theme, size and width, the tools in the launcher, and the live activities beside the camera. Set whether the notch opens on hover or click — every change lands on it immediately.

Everything the notch shows is a setting: Black or Glass, Small to Large, the launcher's tools, live activities, and how it opens. Changes apply as you make them, and Restore Defaults brings the original look back.

The notch stays put. Everything else is yours.

Any tool is a tile.

The launcher holds all thirteen tools in two rows per page. Hold a tile to pin it beside Home, or choose the set in Settings › Home. Three shortcuts stay one click away.

The Arrowslit guide, in one note.

The notch on this page is a working replica of Arrowslit, Jay Gopal’s free, open-source utility for the MacBook notch. The full guide lives with the source.

  1. Open it with a hover over the top centre, or a click. Escape or moving away closes it.
  2. Find a tool in the grid at the top right: thirteen tools, two rows per page. Scroll for page two, or hold a tile to pin it beside Home.
  3. Keep it running: timers, calls, media, downloads and agent work stay beside the camera when the notch is closed.
  4. Make it yours in Settings: Black or Glass, size and width, what stays live, and hover or click.

See it on your own notch.

  1. Download Arrowslit.
  2. Open it from Applications. macOS asks once; allow it in Privacy & Security.
  3. Hover the top centre or click the slit icon. Calendar, Reminders and Camera ask only when you use them.
  4. Make it yours in Settings: theme, size, launcher tools and live activities.
