Privacy Policy

Last updated on 8 November 2026.

Arrowslit is a free, open-source notch app for macOS by Jay Gopal. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry; we do not collect personal data.

On your Mac

Arrowslit keeps your settings, clipboard history and scratchpad on your Mac. Clipboard history lives in memory; only clips you pin are written to disk. Scratchpad notes and agent usage stay local too.

Arrowslit asks for macOS permissions only when you use a feature:

  • Camera — for Camera Mirror. The sensor turns off when the panel closes.
  • Calendars — when you connect Calendar for the agenda and the meeting heads-up.
  • Reminders — when you open Reminders.

Declining a permission disables only that tool.

What the app connects to

  • Updates. Arrowslit checks GitHub for a signed release at launch and once a day. The check is anonymous; an update is downloaded only when you choose to install it.
  • Lyrics (optional). Off by default. When on, the song title, artist, album and length are sent to lrclib.net.
  • Sports (optional). Preview matches are generated locally; live scores use your own BigBallsData key.

GitHub receives your IP address with update checks; its privacy statement covers how GitHub handles it. Nothing else leaves your Mac.

Agent approvals

When you switch on a coding assistant, Arrowslit installs its local hook. Requests travel over a user-only local socket and nothing is auto-allowed. The trust model is documented in the agent approval security review.

This website

This landing page is a static Arrowslit demo. It has no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts; the light or dark choice is saved in your browser. Fonts are served locally. Following a GitHub link sends a request to GitHub. The MacNative, MacBase, TryMacApps, JustMacApps, CodeHype and BetterLaunch badges are stored locally; following their links sends a request to those sites.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy appear on this page with a new date. Questions or reports are welcome on the Arrowslit issue tracker.
